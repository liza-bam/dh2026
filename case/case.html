Digital Hub, 2026

IT support with AI agents. The first decision was where not to use them.

A rethink of the EY Digital Hub I designed in 2019 (https://sandy.tayts.com/dh.html): the same research, the same ten journeys, rebuilt for today.

Role: UX, product and service designer. Service design, agent architecture.

Year: 2026, my own project.

Most IT support needs no AI

I asked one question of each journey: does solving it need a decision?

How it works

What it costs

Technical document, with every agent, rule and number: https://claude.ai/artifact/CS5DidDxcR4oQ1qnekNmPY

Costs: MetricNet benchmarks ($20.61 per contact, 1.3 tickets per user a month, 20 minutes saved per avoided ticket); model costs measured in the demo or assumed. Logic checked against ServiceNow, Moveworks, Salesforce and Microsoft in November 2026. Gartner expects over 40% of agentic AI projects to be canceled by the end of 2027 and warns about "agent washing". Digital Hub uses an agent only where a flow cannot do the job.
