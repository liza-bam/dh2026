# Style rules for everything in this project

- Text on a dark or coloured fill is white (or another light colour). Never dark text on a dark fill. Check every label, badge and chip.
- Text on a light fill is the slate ink #223640 or the secondary colour.
- No arrows in links or buttons.
- No invented jargon or labels; plain words.

- Every employee step in a flow is an action in a channel: a verb (writes, taps, opens) and the channel logo (Teams, phone, Outlook). No thoughts or feelings as steps.
- Flows fit the page width: long flows stack into rows (letter connectors between rows). The full one-row version is kept in case/large/ for a later pop-up.
- One lane "What the employee does and sees": the card he sees, then his action right under it.
